# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination,
and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers through the contact
method in `SECURITY.md`. All reports will be reviewed and handled fairly.
